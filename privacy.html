Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

CG Studios Uploader ("the app") is an internal tool operated by CG Studios Media. It is used only by the owner of the CG Studios Media YouTube channels to upload and manage videos on those channels.

Data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set video titles, descriptions, tags, captions and privacy settings, and read basic information about the owner's own channels and videos.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to upload and manage videos on the owner's own channels. OAuth tokens are stored privately by the owner and are never shared, sold or used for advertising. The app does not collect data from other users or viewers.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
